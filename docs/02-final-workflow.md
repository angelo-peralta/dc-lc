# 02. Final Workflow

## Shared Workflow

1. Student selects service.
2. Student completes public form.
3. Student uploads required documents.
4. System generates tracking number.
5. Service Coordinator reviews requirements.
6. If incomplete, return for correction.
7. If complete, student is instructed to pay at BAO.
8. Student uploads official receipt.
9. Service Coordinator verifies receipt.
10. Service Coordinator assigns specialist.
11. Specialist accepts or declines.
12. Specialist processes case.
13. Specialist uploads output.
14. CPRINT Director reviews.
15. If revision is needed, return to specialist.
16. Director approves.
17. Student completes evaluation.
18. Final output download is enabled.
19. Case is completed.
20. Coordinator may archive/export.
21. Export starts 3-year retention.

## Withdrawal
Student may withdraw only before payment.

## Reopening
Completed/closed cases may be reopened by authorized staff. A reason, user, timestamp, and audit event are required.

## Student Edits
Students may edit permitted submitted information. Every change must record old value, new value, actor, and timestamp.
