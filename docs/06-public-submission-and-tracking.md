# 06. Public Submission and Tracking

## Landing Page
Primary actions:
- Data Consultation
- Language Editing
- Track Submission

## No Student Account
Students do not register or log in.

## Tracking Number
One tracking number is issued after submission.

Recommended format should contain a random component because the tracking number alone is used for access.

Examples:
- `DC-2026-K7M4Q9X2`
- `LE-2026-P8R2N6TW`

## Tracking Portal
May show:
- service;
- student name;
- research title;
- current status;
- timeline;
- deficiencies;
- uploaded files;
- payment-proof status;
- assigned consultant/editor;
- evaluation requirement;
- released output.

## Student Edits
Students may update permitted case information. Changes must be audited.

## Future Feature
Built-in messaging is future/secondary priority. Version 1 may rely on status, remarks, correction requests, and email notifications.
