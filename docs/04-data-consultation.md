# 04. Data Consultation Module

## Applicant
Undergraduate student / project leader only. No group-member records are required.

## Required Applicant Information
- Student Number
- First Name
- Middle Name
- Last Name
- School / College
- Program
- Year Level
- Institutional Email
- Contact Number
- Faculty Adviser
- Research Title

## Required Documents
- Research Manuscript
- Clean / Anonymized Dataset
- Other Supporting Document

Admin may add future requirement types.

## Declarations
Student confirms dataset is clean and anonymized/de-identified and information is accurate.

## Payment
After requirements are complete, student pays PHP 1,650 at BAO, uploads official receipt, and DC Coordinator verifies it.

## Assignment
DC Coordinator assigns a Data Consultant only after verified payment proof.

Consultant must Accept or Decline with reason.

## Processing Period
15 working days begin when the consultant is assigned. Weekends are excluded.

Suggested fields:
- assigned_at
- processing_started_at
- due_at
- completed_at

## Agreement
DC-2 is digitized, preserving client, consultant, scope, confidentiality, authorship, payment, and acceptance timestamps.

## Continuing Consultation
The case may remain active according to the consultation agreement until final manuscript submission to the program office.

Student pays only once for the case.

## Outputs
Support multiple versions and preserve prior versions.

## Final Review
CPRINT Director reviews output before release.

## Evaluation
Mandatory before final output download.
