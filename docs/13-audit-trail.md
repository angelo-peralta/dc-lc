# 13. Audit Trail

## Goal
Provide enough history to trace what happened without logging every routine page view.

## Track
- CASE_CREATED
- CASE_SUBMITTED
- CASE_EDITED
- REQUIREMENT_MARKED_COMPLETE
- REQUIREMENT_MARKED_DEFICIENT
- CORRECTION_UPLOADED
- FILE_UPLOADED
- FILE_DOWNLOADED
- FILE_SUPERSEDED
- PAYMENT_PROOF_SUBMITTED
- PAYMENT_PROOF_VERIFIED
- PAYMENT_PROOF_REJECTED
- CONSULTANT_ASSIGNED
- CONSULTANT_ACCEPTED
- CONSULTANT_DECLINED
- CONSULTANT_REASSIGNED
- EDITOR_ASSIGNED
- EDITOR_ACCEPTED
- EDITOR_DECLINED
- EDITOR_REASSIGNED
- OUTPUT_UPLOADED
- REVISION_REQUESTED
- DIRECTOR_APPROVED
- EVALUATION_SUBMITTED
- FINAL_OUTPUT_DOWNLOADED
- CASE_WITHDRAWN
- CASE_REOPENED
- CASE_COMPLETED
- CASE_ARCHIVED
- ARCHIVE_EXPORTED
- RETENTION_EXPIRED
- FILES_PURGED

## Student Edits
Always record old value, new value, actor, and timestamp.

## Downloads
Log important downloads such as datasets, outputs, receipts, and archive ZIPs.
